Common Questions

Before you get in touch.

Will it damage my paintwork or the metal underneath?

Laser cleaning removes contamination layer by layer, without cutting into sound material beneath — that's the main advantage over grinding or sandblasting. We'll confirm suitability for your specific item during the assessment before any work happens.

How much will it cost?

Every job is priced individually based on size, material and condition — there's no fixed rate card. The free assessment gives you a real quote before you commit to anything.

Do I need to bring it to you?

No — we're mobile and bring the equipment to you, across Glasgow and Central Scotland.

What if laser cleaning isn't right for my item?

We'll tell you honestly during the assessment. No pressure, no obligation to book anything.

How long does a job take?

Depends on size and condition — we'll give you a realistic timeframe as part of your assessment, not a guess upfront.

I'm outside Central Scotland — should I still get in touch?

Yes — we're expanding our service area, and knowing where demand is helps us plan where to go next.
